## Brand DNA

- confident monochrome, not generic AI gradients;
- type carries hierarchy; decoration does not;
- warm paper and precise ink;
- thin separators and deliberate alignment;
- restrained accent reserved for meaning;
- concise, direct voice with no chatbot enthusiasm.

## Product DNA

- mathematics appears immediately;
- dense enough for serious work, never cramped;
- fast local feedback;
- visible causal state changes;
- controls behave like an instrument;
- technical detail is available on demand, not smeared over the core experience.

## Learning DNA

- diagnose the first broken inference;
- teach beside the learner's own work;
- preserve learner authorship;
- progressively withdraw help;
- generate a fresh challenge after repair;
- measure only what the page actually observed;
- fail closed when mathematical certainty is unavailable.
