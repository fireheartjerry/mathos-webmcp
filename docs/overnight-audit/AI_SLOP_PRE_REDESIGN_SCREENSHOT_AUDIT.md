## Pre-redesign component verdict

| Component | Role then | Slop signal | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | Brand/navigation | Generic rounded nav treatment | Rebuild on shared editorial grid. |
| Hero | Marketing claim | Cream/serif/sage bundle and familiar conversion skeleton | Replace with direct falsifiable claim. |
| Feature cards | Explain product | Equal-weight card soup | Remove. |
| Video lesson | “Personalization” proof | Generated spectacle and unreliable empty states | Remove from rescue candidate. |
| Scratchpad | Main work | Buried inside layered panels | Promote to open work field. |
| Diagnosis | Error feedback | One card among many | Make first-break surface dominant. |
| Agent tools | Integration proof | Raw tool/dev language permanently visible | Collapse into explicit inspector. |
| Transfer | Learning evidence | Another success card | Make assistance closure and fresh problem explicit. |
| Receipt | Outcome | Risks badge/mastery language | Use bounded evidence with limitations. |

## Baseline verdict

The original product was not merely “too decorative.” It made the wrong thing visually important: presentation systems, cards, and generated lesson media outweighed the core trust mechanism. The rescue therefore required product subtraction and a new evidence hierarchy, not a palette swap.
