## Why the winner survives the backend-MCP attack

The agent does not upload a completed answer to a remote service. It reads the exact unfinished
artifact already in the tab, requests page-owned symbolic checks, and writes bounded teaching
beside the learner's actual line. The learner can edit concurrently; revisions and visible
activity make that collaboration auditable. The same page immediately generates and locks an
unaided second round.

A backend could expose algebra. It cannot naturally own this live DOM-adjacent learning state,
the learner's current selection and consent, visible verdict mutation, same-tab revision race, or
the transition into an unaided browser round. That irreducibility is the product.

## Frozen promise

> Mathos checks the first place your reasoning stopped following, lets an agent teach beside that
> exact line without doing the work for you, then gives you a fresh unaided problem to see whether
> the idea carries.

No further product branching is authorized for this rescue.
